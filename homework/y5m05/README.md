## What is in it

- **A** Number lines: count on and back past zero in equal steps (5Nc.01), two jumps to
  draw, twelve calculations with one negative number, getting bigger (5Ni.01).
- **B** The English: a Word help box, words to number sentence (temperature, floors
  below the ground, money owed, "subtract 12 from 5"), the difference between.
- **C** A temperature table (made-up numbers, five places) and estimate-then-calculate.
- **D** Mr. David's three wrong answers to correct and explain; write a story for a
  number sentence.
